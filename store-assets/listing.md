## Description
htmlEdit lets you open an HTML file and edit it visually — exactly as it looks — instead of digging through code.

Click any text and start typing. Format with a simple toolbar: bold, italic, underline, headings, lists and links. Press ⌘S and your changes save straight back to the file.

No code. No re-prompting an AI to "change this bit." No cloud. Just point, click and type.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Edit the live, rendered page — what you see is what you get
• A clean, distraction-free toolbar for everyday formatting
• Opens and saves standard .html files anywhere on your Mac
• Completely private — nothing leaves your device, no account, no tracking
• Fast and lightweight

PERFECT FOR
• Tidying up documents and pages generated by AI tools
• Quick edits to reports, memos and one-page sites
• Anyone who wants to change words on a web page without touching the code

htmlEdit does one thing well: makes editing HTML feel like editing a document.
