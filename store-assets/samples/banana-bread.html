Brown Butter Banana Bread

Simple, forgiving, and better the next day.
Prep 15 minBake 55 min1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 110g butter, browned and cooled
  • 150g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs · 1 tsp vanilla
  • 220g plain flour · 1 tsp baking soda · pinch of salt

Method

  1. Heat oven to 175°C and line a loaf tin.
  2. Whisk butter and sugar, then beat in eggs, vanilla and bananas.
  3. Fold in the dry ingredients until just combined.
  4. Pour in and bake 50–55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.

Tip

Let it cool fully before slicing — it firms up and the flavour deepens.
