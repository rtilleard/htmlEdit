Product Brief

Orchard — Quick Capture

A faster way to save ideas before they disappear.
Owner
Product Team
Status
In review
Target
Q3 release

The problem

People lose good ideas because capturing them takes too many steps. By the time the right app is open, the thought is gone.

What we're building

  • One keystroke to capture from anywhere
  • Automatic sorting into projects
  • Works fully offline, syncs when you're back online

Success looks like

A new note takes under two seconds, and nothing is ever lost. We'll measure capture time and weekly active note-takers.
